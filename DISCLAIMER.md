## You must own the game

Use this project only with a copy of *The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the
Kingdom* that you bought, and with keys and system files dumped from your own
Nintendo Switch. Do not download or share game files, keys, firmware, or saves.
Whether dumping your own game is legal depends on where you live; check and
follow your local laws.

## No game content

This repository contains no game program, art, text, audio, shaders, keys,
ROMs, saves, firmware, extracted or rewritten game modules, or built
applications. The build scripts require *your* game file and *your* keys, and
the resulting app contains your game data. **Do not share the built app.**

## Saves and game behavior

The mods change gameplay. Back up your saves and use a copied profile for
testing. A mod can alter a save in ways the original game does not. Use at your
own risk.

## Battery mod status

The unlimited-battery switch is gauge-verified, not device-tested. It is
labelled beta until a real Zonai device has been exercised with the switch on.
Do not rely on it for a save you care about.
